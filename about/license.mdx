## What is CC0 1.0 Universal?

CC0 1.0 Universal is a public domain dedication that allows creators to waive all their copyright and related rights in their works to the fullest extent allowed by law.

<Info>
  The CC0 1.0 Universal license means you can use this data for **any purpose** without restriction, including commercial use, without needing to ask permission.
</Info>
