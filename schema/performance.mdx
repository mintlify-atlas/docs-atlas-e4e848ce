## Overview

Performance tables capture the on-track action, including race results, qualifying times, lap-by-lap data, and pit stop information. These tables form the core analytical dataset for Formula 1 performance analysis.

## results.csv

The primary table containing race results, finishing positions, and performance statistics for each driver in each race.

### Schema

<ParamField path="resultId" type="integer" required>
  Unique identifier for each result. Primary key.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="raceId" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key to `races.csv`.
  
  **Example**: `18`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="driverId" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key to `drivers.csv`.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="constructorId" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key to `constructors.csv`.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="number" type="integer">
  Driver's car number for this race. May be `\N` for older races.
  
  **Example**: `44`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="grid" type="integer" required>
  Starting grid position (1 = pole position, 0 = pitlane start).
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="position" type="integer">
  Finishing position. May be `\N` if driver did not finish.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="positionText" type="string" required>
  Text representation of position. May be "R" (retired), "D" (disqualified), "E" (excluded), "W" (withdrawn), "F" (failed to qualify), "N" (not classified), or a number.
  
  **Example**: `"1"` or `"R"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="positionOrder" type="integer" required>
  Order of finish for sorting purposes. Finishers come before non-finishers.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="points" type="float" required>
  Championship points scored in this race.
  
  **Example**: `10.0`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="laps" type="integer" required>
  Number of laps completed.
  
  **Example**: `58`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="time" type="string">
  Total race time for winner, or gap to winner (e.g., "+5.478"). May be `\N` for DNF.
  
  **Example**: `"1:34:50.616"` or `"+5.478"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="milliseconds" type="integer">
  Total race time in milliseconds. May be `\N` for DNF.
  
  **Example**: `5690616`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fastestLap" type="integer">
  Lap number of driver's fastest lap. May be `\N`.
  
  **Example**: `39`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="rank" type="integer">
  Rank of this driver's fastest lap compared to all drivers (1 = fastest). May be `\N`.
  
  **Example**: `2`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fastestLapTime" type="string">
  Time of the fastest lap in M:SS.mmm format. May be `\N`.
  
  **Example**: `"1:27.452"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fastestLapSpeed" type="float">
  Speed of fastest lap in km/h. May be `\N`.
  
  **Example**: `218.3`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="statusId" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key to `status.csv` indicating finish status.
  
  **Example**: `1` (Finished)
</ParamField>

### Sample Data

| resultId | raceId | driverId | constructorId | grid | position | points | laps | time | fastestLapTime |
|----------|--------|----------|---------------|------|----------|--------|------|------|----------------|
| 1 | 18 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 10 | 58 | 1:34:50.616 | 1:27.452 |
| 2 | 18 | 2 | 2 | 5 | 2 | 8 | 58 | +5.478 | 1:27.739 |
| 3 | 18 | 3 | 3 | 7 | 3 | 6 | 58 | +8.163 | 1:28.090 |

<Note>
**Time Format**: The `time` field shows absolute time for P1 and relative time (gap) for all other finishers. DNF entries have `\N`.
</Note>

## status.csv

Lookup table for race finishing status codes.

### Schema

<ParamField path="statusId" type="integer" required>
  Unique status identifier. Primary key.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="status" type="string" required>
  Description of the status.
  
  **Example**: `"Finished"` or `"Engine"`
</ParamField>

### Sample Data

| statusId | status |
|----------|--------|
| 1 | Finished |
| 2 | Disqualified |
| 3 | Accident |
| 4 | Collision |
| 5 | Engine |
| 6 | Gearbox |
| 7 | Transmission |
| 8 | Clutch |
| 9 | Hydraulics |

### Common Status Codes

- **1** - Finished (completed the race)
- **2** - Disqualified
- **3** - Accident
- **4** - Collision
- **5** - Engine (engine failure)
- **6** - Gearbox (gearbox failure)
- **11** - +1 Lap (finished but 1 lap down)
- **12** - +2 Laps
- **13** - +3 Laps

## Example Queries

### Race Winner with Full Details
```sql
SELECT 
    d.forename || ' ' || d.surname as driver,
    c.name as constructor,
    res.grid,
    res.laps,
    res.time,
    res.fastestLapTime,
    res.points
FROM results res
JOIN drivers d ON res.driverId = d.driverId
JOIN constructors c ON res.constructorId = c.constructorId
WHERE res.raceId = 1061 AND res.position = 1
```

### Fastest Pit Stop in a Race
```sql
SELECT 
    d.code,
    c.name as team,
    ps.stop,
    ps.lap,
    ps.duration
FROM pit_stops ps
JOIN drivers d ON ps.driverId = d.driverId
JOIN results r ON ps.driverId = r.driverId AND ps.raceId = r.raceId
JOIN constructors c ON r.constructorId = c.constructorId
WHERE ps.raceId = 258
ORDER BY ps.milliseconds
LIMIT 1
```

### Qualifying Progression
```sql
SELECT 
    d.code,
    q.position,
    q.q1,
    q.q2,
    q.q3
FROM qualifying q
JOIN drivers d ON q.driverId = d.driverId
WHERE q.raceId = 18
ORDER BY q.position
```
