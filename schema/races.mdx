## Overview

The race-related tables form the core structure of the dataset, defining when and where Formula 1 races occurred. These three tables work together to provide complete context for every Grand Prix event.

## races.csv

The main table containing individual race events, including session schedules and timing information.

### Schema

<ParamField path="raceId" type="integer" required>
  Unique identifier for each race. Primary key.
  
  **Example**: `1061`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="year" type="integer" required>
  The year of the race. Links to `seasons.year`.
  
  **Example**: `2009`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="round" type="integer" required>
  The round number of the race in the season (1-based).
  
  **Example**: `1` (first race of the season)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="circuitId" type="integer" required>
  Foreign key to `circuits.csv`. Identifies which circuit hosted the race.
  
  **Example**: `1` (Albert Park)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  The official name of the Grand Prix.
  
  **Example**: `"Australian Grand Prix"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="date" type="date" required>
  The date of the race in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  
  **Example**: `"2009-03-29"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="time" type="time">
  The time of the race start in HH:MM:SS format (UTC). May be `\N` for older races.
  
  **Example**: `"06:00:00"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="url" type="string">
  Wikipedia URL for the race event.
  
  **Example**: `"http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009_Australian_Grand_Prix"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fp1_date" type="date">
  Date of Free Practice 1 session. May be `\N` for older races.
  
  **Example**: `"2024-03-07"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fp1_time" type="time">
  Time of Free Practice 1 session (UTC). May be `\N` for older races.
  
  **Example**: `"11:30:00"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fp2_date" type="date">
  Date of Free Practice 2 session. May be `\N` for older races.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fp2_time" type="time">
  Time of Free Practice 2 session (UTC). May be `\N` for older races.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fp3_date" type="date">
  Date of Free Practice 3 session. May be `\N` for older races or sprint weekends.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="fp3_time" type="time">
  Time of Free Practice 3 session (UTC). May be `\N` for older races or sprint weekends.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="quali_date" type="date">
  Date of Qualifying session. May be `\N` for older races.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="quali_time" type="time">
  Time of Qualifying session (UTC). May be `\N` for older races.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="sprint_date" type="date">
  Date of Sprint Race. Only populated for races with sprint format (2021+).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="sprint_time" type="time">
  Time of Sprint Race (UTC). Only populated for races with sprint format (2021+).
</ParamField>

### Sample Data

| raceId | year | round | circuitId | name | date | time |
|--------|------|-------|-----------|------|------|------|
| 1 | 2009 | 1 | 1 | Australian Grand Prix | 2009-03-29 | 06:00:00 |
| 2 | 2009 | 2 | 2 | Malaysian Grand Prix | 2009-04-05 | 09:00:00 |
| 3 | 2009 | 3 | 17 | Chinese Grand Prix | 2009-04-19 | 07:00:00 |

<Note>
**Session Data Availability**: Free practice and qualifying session dates/times are more complete for recent seasons (2015+). Older races may have `\N` values for these fields.
</Note>

## circuits.csv

### Schema

<ParamField path="circuitId" type="integer" required>
  Unique identifier for each circuit. Primary key.
  
  **Example**: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="circuitRef" type="string" required>

  **Example**: `"albert_park"`

  Official name of the circuit.
  
  **Example**: `"Albert Park Grand Prix Circuit"`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="location" type="string" required>
  City or area where the circuit is located.

</ParamField>

  **Example**: `"Australia"`

## Relationships Between Tables

```
seasons (year)
    ↓
races (year) → circuits (circuitId)
    ↓
[Links to all performance tables]
```

### Key Relationships

1. **seasons → races**: Each race belongs to a season via the `year` field
2. **races → circuits**: Each race is held at a specific circuit via `circuitId`
3. **races → results/qualifying/etc**: Each race has multiple performance records

### Example Query: Get All Races at a Circuit

```sql
SELECT r.year, r.round, r.name, r.date, c.name as circuit_name
FROM races r
JOIN circuits c ON r.circuitId = c.circuitId
WHERE c.circuitRef = 'monaco'
ORDER BY r.year, r.round
```

### Example Query: Get Full Race Weekend Schedule

```sql
SELECT 
    r.name,
    r.date as race_date,
    r.time as race_time,
    r.fp1_date,
    r.fp1_time,
    r.fp2_date,
    r.fp2_time,
    r.fp3_date,
    r.fp3_time,
    r.quali_date,
    r.quali_time,
    r.sprint_date,
    r.sprint_time,
    c.name as circuit,
    c.location,
    c.country
FROM races r
JOIN circuits c ON r.circuitId = c.circuitId
WHERE r.year = 2024
ORDER BY r.round
```

## Data Usage Tips

<Note>
**Sprint Weekend Format**: On sprint weekends, FP3 is replaced by the Sprint Race. Check `sprint_date` to identify sprint weekends.
</Note>

<Note>
**Historical Changes**: Circuit configurations and names have changed over time. The same `circuitId` may represent different layouts across different eras.
</Note>

<Note>
**Time Zones**: All times are stored in UTC. Convert to local time using the circuit's `lat`/`lng` coordinates.
</Note>
